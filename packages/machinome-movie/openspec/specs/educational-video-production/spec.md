# educational-video-production Specification

## Purpose

Support project-authored, inspectable local narrated films of an existing
posed Machinome simulation, with reproducible presentation and production
evidence separate from publication.

Administrative note (29 September 2026): This Purpose was added for baseline
sync after the first rehearsal was implemented and adopted during
implementation. It does not change the original requirements, scenarios,
evidence, or adoption chronology, and does not claim prior ratification.
## Requirements
### Requirement: The author can inspect and edit one screenplay

An episode SHALL keep narration, visual intent and timed presentation cues in
human-readable project-owned source, with technical presets separately readable.
Reading an episode MUST NOT require executing program code. A movie document
(`machinome-movie/0.2`) SHALL be accepted as the source instead: it is data,
generated by the project's declarations, holding each chapter's narration
beside the shots it spans, and the tool MUST NOT execute anything to read it;
the project's stage modules it names are bundled to draw the film, never run
to read it. A YAML episode SHALL behave as a movie whose every shot is one
chapter with that shot's narration. Both SHALL be drawn through sets: a
document through the sets its stage block lists, an episode through the sets
the tool ships for episodes.

#### Scenario: Revise a lesson

- **WHEN** the author opens the sawmill episode
- **THEN** each shot shows its narration beside its visual direction and cues
- **AND** those same sources are consumed by production without a second script.

#### Scenario: Revise a lesson written as a movie

- **WHEN** the author reads a movie document
- **THEN** each chapter shows its narration beside the shots it spans, the
  elements they place in which slots of which sets, their directions and cues
- **AND** that same document is consumed by production without a second script.

#### Scenario: Refuse ambiguous source

- **WHEN** source contains duplicate keys, aliases or executable/custom types
- **THEN** production fails with a source error rather than guessing intent.

### Requirement: Invalid production inputs are refused before capture

Production SHALL check episode timing, model duration and pinned source revision,
named camera/layer references and evidence file existence before rendering.

#### Scenario: Detect a stale sawmill export

- **WHEN** the export duration or recorded model revision differs from production settings
- **THEN** production fails before rendering frames from that export.

#### Scenario: Detect incomplete direction

- **WHEN** shots overlap or leave a gap, or reference an unknown layer or camera
- **THEN** production identifies the invalid input rather than silently omitting it.

### Requirement: Presentation follows the authored model state

The film SHALL present the existing posed simulation at the authored times,
viewpoints and visibility states, independently of capture speed. The tool MUST
NOT create replacement mechanical behavior or imply support for stateful replay.

#### Scenario: Explain a stroke slowly

- **WHEN** a shot maps its video interval to a shorter model interval or explicit model-time keys
- **THEN** frames follow that mapping without advancing by wall-clock time.

#### Scenario: Restore the whole assembly

- **WHEN** a reveal shot is followed by a shot with no hidden layers
- **THEN** the previously hidden parts are visible again.

#### Scenario: Revisit a pose

- **WHEN** the tested renderer returns to an identical time and presentation after another shot
- **THEN** it restores identical pixels in that same rendering environment.

### Requirement: Narration is generated and reusable

The author SHALL be able to generate synthetic narration using the configured
ElevenLabs voice and reuse unchanged generated speech without another paid request.
Credentials MUST remain outside project sources, browser content and build records.
Narration SHALL be generated as one take per chapter; a YAML episode's chapters
are its shots, so its cached takes keep their keys and remain reusable.

#### Scenario: Change camera timing only

- **WHEN** presentation is revised but cached speech inputs are unchanged
- **THEN** production reuses the verified cached segments and their alignment.

#### Scenario: Existing episodes keep their takes

- **WHEN** a YAML episode whose takes were cached before chapters existed is
  checked, previewed or rendered
- **THEN** the same cached takes are found and used, and nothing is requested
  from the provider.

#### Scenario: A sentence is too long

- **WHEN** measured speech exceeds its available shot interval, or for a movie
  its chapter's interval
- **THEN** production reports the required duration and retains the generated segment
- **AND** it does not truncate or silently stretch the narration.

### Requirement: Captions and teaching cues follow the lesson

The film SHALL show authored explanatory labels with the posed 3D animation.
A narrated render SHALL provide a same-basename UTF-8 SRT beside the MP4,
timed from verified speech alignment and retaining the written project name
when a pronunciation alias is spoken. The narration transcript MUST NOT be
burned into film frames or embedded in the MP4.

#### Scenario: Explain cut and advance

- **WHEN** the sawmill episode marks cut/advance phases around measured spoken words
- **THEN** corresponding model poses and explanatory labels follow the authored phase mapping
- **AND** the external SRT follows generated speech alignment rather than reading-speed estimates.

### Requirement: An author can inspect a local rehearsal

The workflow SHALL produce still previews and a narrated local MP4 at the
requested dimensions, duration and render frame rate, with reusable caption
sidecars. The global output grid requires an integral frame count
`N = durationSeconds × render FPS`; its frames are indexed `0..N-1` at times
`index / render FPS`. Shot boundaries may fall between these frame instants.

#### Scenario: Render a valid episode

- **WHEN** a valid screenplay authored without in-film credits and its cached narration are rendered
- **THEN** the local MP4 has `N` frames at the requested dimensions and render FPS, with narration audio
- **AND** a same-basename SRT sits beside it, while no narration transcript or authored credit appears in its picture.

#### Scenario: Inspect before a full render

- **WHEN** the author requests a still preview or reduced silent render
- **THEN** the workflow produces review media without generating speech.

### Requirement: Credits and publication gaps are explicit

The author SHALL keep source-project attribution, copyright and licence text,
and public simulation/source links in production-side credits and the draft
or generated upload description, outside the film picture, narration and
subtitle transcript. Unresolved public URLs SHALL remain marked placeholders
and MUST NOT be replaced with invented links. Missing public URLs SHALL make
`releaseReady` false. Shown-picture media also remain subject to the separate
credit, rights, committed-file and `{{pictureCredits}}` description-template
gates. Videomaker records these facts without judging rights or checking live
links. Building a video MUST NOT upload or publish it.

#### Scenario: Review a rehearsal before the simulation is public

- **WHEN** public simulation or source URLs are unresolved
- **THEN** a local rehearsal can still be produced, with attribution and licence text in its description rather than its picture, narration or subtitles
- **AND** generated release copy marks missing links and `releaseReady` is false.

### Requirement: Production leaves inspectable evidence

Generated media SHALL stay outside committed source in the consuming project's
build directory. Production SHALL record resolved source inputs, model/viewer and
audio identities, output checks and rendering environment for the rehearsal.

#### Scenario: Trace the first video

- **WHEN** an author inspects the completed build manifest
- **THEN** it identifies the screenplay, model revision, actual asset/audio hashes,
  rendering settings and encoded media properties used for that build.

### Requirement: The film carries no progress indicator

A rendered film SHALL NOT draw any indicator of how far through the film a
frame is: no progress bar, track, fill, counter or other film-wide position
mark, in any format, on any set and on any frame. Showing position is the job
of the player. A masthead and a series label are not position indicators and
remain wherever a set draws them; the shipped paper sets keep theirs. No
episode, document or production field SHALL turn a progress indicator on.
The removal applies to every existing episode alike. Earlier requirements
that promise unchanged frames compare the presence and absence of their own
feature on the same sets; they do not compare against frames rendered before
this removal.

#### Scenario: The sawmill film plays without a bar

- **WHEN** Leonardo's `one-wheel-two-jobs` is rendered
- **THEN** no frame, from the first fade-in frame to the last fade-out frame
  and through every cut and transition, shows a line or bar along its bottom
  edge, while `MACHINOME` and the series label appear in the masthead

#### Scenario: A movie on its own template

- **WHEN** Leonardo's *Cut and Advance* is rendered from its movie document
- **THEN** no frame shows any mark of the film's position, and the only
  chrome is what the sets `Leonardo` and `LeonardoSplit` draw

#### Scenario: Asking for the rule back

- **WHEN** a movie document's `style` carries a `progress` field
- **THEN** it is refused as a field the schema does not know, naming `style`
  and `progress`

### Requirement: Provenance says whether each source tree was dirty

Wherever production records a Git head, it SHALL record beside it whether
that work tree was dirty: a tracked file changed, staged or not, or an
untracked path Git does not ignore. The trees are the project repository
(`projectHead`, `projectDirty`) and Videomaker's own checkout
(`workflowHead`, `workflowDirty`). `preview` and `render` SHALL read both
trees once, before the first frame, and use that reading in every record
they write: the render manifest's `provenance`, each captured shot
generation's `index.provenance`, and the preview's `report.json`. `check`,
`preview` and `render` SHALL each print a `Project tree:` and a
`Videomaker tree:` line: `clean at` the head, or `dirty at` the command,
how many paths differ from the head, at most three of them by name, and
what that means for the command's record. A dirty tree MUST NOT be refused.

The render manifest SHALL list in `releaseIssues` every reason it is not
release-ready, in words: a missing description template, each unresolved
public link, each picture release issue, and each tree dirty at render,
naming the tree, the head and the paths. `releaseReady` SHALL be true
exactly when `releaseIssues` is empty. Whether a tree was dirty MUST NOT
enter the shot cache's common key or any shot's key.

#### Scenario: A render from a project tree with an untracked file
- **WHEN** the author renders a film whose project tree holds an untracked,
  unignored `notes.txt`
- **THEN** `render` prints `Project tree: dirty at render, 1 path differs
  from its head <head>` naming `notes.txt`, the manifest records
  `projectDirty: true` beside `projectHead`, `releaseReady` is false and
  `releaseIssues` names the project tree, its head and `notes.txt`.

#### Scenario: A render from a clean project tree
- **WHEN** the project tree has no change and no unignored untracked path
- **THEN** `render` prints `Project tree: clean at <head>`, the manifest and
  each captured shot's provenance record `projectDirty: false`, and no
  reason in `releaseIssues` names the project tree.

#### Scenario: A tracked file changed before check and preview
- **WHEN** a tracked file of the project is modified and the author runs
  `check` and then `preview`
- **THEN** each prints `Project tree: dirty at` its command, naming the
  file, and the preview's `report.json` records `projectDirty: true`.

#### Scenario: Dirt is not a picture input
- **WHEN** a film rendered from a clean tree is rendered again, unchanged,
  after an untracked file appears in the project
- **THEN** the common key is the same, every shot is reused and no frame is
  captured, and only the provenance and the release record change.
### Requirement: The renderer serves the export files the viewer reads, and only those

`preview` and `render` SHALL serve to the stage, from the model's export,
exactly the files the viewer reads: the `model` reference of every node of
the manifest's `root` tree and the `model` of every entry of a node's
`markings` list, as the framework's export document defines them, each once.
They MUST NOT serve or open any other string of the manifest, whatever its
extension: the printed-piece inventory (`pieces[].models`, `pieces[].sources`)
and every provenance string are not files the viewer reads. Before a browser
opens, a file the viewer reads that the export does not hold, or that lies
outside the export directory, SHALL be refused naming its field path in the
manifest, the node it belongs to and the path the field gives, and saying
that the model must be exported again. The URLs served for an export whose
manifest holds no other file-like string SHALL be those served before, in
the same order, with the same hashes.

#### Scenario: A provenance source the export does not hold

- **WHEN** the fixture movie `minimal` is previewed and rendered over an
  export whose `pieces[0].sources` names `STLs/absent.stl`, which exists
  nowhere
- **THEN** both commands succeed, and `STLs/absent.stl` is neither served
  nor opened.

#### Scenario: A model file lost from the export

- **WHEN** the export's manifest gives the part `Plank` the model
  `models/models/lost.stl`, which the export does not hold
- **THEN** `preview` and `render` are refused before a browser opens,
  naming `root.children[2].children[0].model`, `Plank`,
  `models/models/lost.stl` and that the model must be exported again.

#### Scenario: A marking's artifact lost from the export

- **WHEN** a marking's artifact named in the counter's manifest is absent
  from its export
- **THEN** the refusal names that marking's field,
  `root.children[…].markings[0].model`, and its path.

#### Scenario: A model outside the export

- **WHEN** a node's `model` resolves outside the export directory
- **THEN** it is refused naming the field, the node and the path, and
  nothing outside the export is served.

#### Scenario: The served set of a real export is unchanged

- **WHEN** the files of the fixture exports `seesaw` and `counter` are
  collected
- **THEN** they are the distinct `root` model and marking paths in the
  manifest's depth-first order, the same URLs in the same order as the
  extension walk served before this change.
