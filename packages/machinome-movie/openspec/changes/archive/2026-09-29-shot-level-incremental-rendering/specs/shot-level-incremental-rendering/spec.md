## Purpose

Let Leonardo correct one part of a real film without recapturing unrelated shots, while ensuring every reused frame is the frame a fresh render would make.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Unchanged shots reuse verified frames

`render` SHALL reuse the complete output-frame set of a shot only when its effective visual inputs and global output-frame positions are unchanged and its cached frame set is complete and verified. It SHALL capture invalidated shot sets again. The cache unit is the **whole shot**, including its incoming transition; no promise is made to reuse only a transition's unaffected suffix. A local edit SHALL NOT invalidate an unrelated shot merely because both shots share the episode or because unused picture/media metadata changed. Changes to common viewer/model/renderer/chrome inputs SHALL invalidate every affected shot, even if that means the whole film.

#### Scenario: Correct one shot with a following join
- **WHEN** Leonardo's `demo-incremental-rendering` changes only the camera or title of its middle shot, followed by a shot with an incoming transition
- **THEN** the edited middle shot and the complete following shot are captured again, other independently unchanged shots reuse their verified frames, and the manifest reports those counts by shot.

#### Scenario: Change an unused or unrelated picture
- **WHEN** the bytes of a picture used only by one shot change, or an unused picture declaration changes
- **THEN** only shots whose own or outgoing-transition pictures reach those bytes recapture; a wholly unused picture declaration causes no PNG recapture, though current validation and release metadata still update.

#### Scenario: Change a common film input
- **WHEN** the viewer bundle, model export asset, renderer/page behavior, effective film-wide chrome, output geometry/FPS, or film duration changes
- **THEN** no shot is reused under an old common input; a duration change in particular updates the progress rule on all frames.

### Requirement: Global timing and joins determine cache identity

The shot reuse decision SHALL reflect the exact ordered global output frames `n/fps` assigned to that shot and the visible result of each corresponding timeline request, including any outgoing continuation during an incoming transition. It MUST NOT treat unchanged shot-local YAML or locally rounded frame numbers as sufficient proof of reuse. A changed incoming transition SHALL invalidate its incoming shot; a changed outgoing shot SHALL invalidate a following incoming shot when that transition shows it. An ordinary cut SHALL NOT create an outgoing-frame dependency.

#### Scenario: Fractional boundary on the global grid
- **WHEN** a shot begins between output-frame instants and its start or cue timing is edited
- **THEN** reuse is decided from the actual global indices and instants that are filmed, and the incremental frames exactly match a fresh render at each index.

#### Scenario: Outgoing picture continues into the next shot
- **WHEN** an outgoing model, overlay, source panel or clip changes and the next shot's transition still shows it past its nominal end
- **THEN** the next shot is recaptured as one unit and its transition frames show the edited outgoing state at the same film instants as a fresh render.

### Requirement: Measured words and audio retain their existing authority

`render` SHALL resolve measured word/source-emphasis timing from the same verified cached speech takes as before, validate output-frame word fit, and bind those takes to any shot frames that visibly depend on them. A cache hit MUST NOT hide a missing or changed take, purchase speech, or bypass a timing refusal. Narration or SRT changes with no visual effect MAY reuse PNGs, but the final audio, subtitles and current output provenance MUST reflect the current inputs.

#### Scenario: Measured take changes
- **WHEN** a cached speech take or its measured alignment changes for a shot with spoken reveal or source emphasis
- **THEN** its dependent shot frames are recaptured or rendering refuses as the existing timing rules require; no old word positions are silently reused.

#### Scenario: Audio-only edit
- **WHEN** narration changes in a shot without measured visual cues, with valid current audio available
- **THEN** its unchanged PNGs may be reused, but the output mix/subtitles and manifest belong to the current narration; no speech is generated by `render`.

### Requirement: Interrupted or damaged caches never count as completed shots

`render` SHALL reuse only a complete shot generation with an atomic completion record and verified frame count, global indices, dimensions and byte hashes. A missing, partial, malformed or mismatched generation MUST be treated as a cache miss and recaptured or yield a clear render failure; a nonempty PNG alone is not proof. A failed shot capture MUST NOT invalidate healthy completed generations or cause an incomplete generation to be used by an encoder. The new cache SHALL NOT silently import pre-change whole-episode frame directories.

#### Scenario: Resume after interruption
- **WHEN** capture stops after one shot has completed and the next has only some PNGs
- **THEN** the completed shot may be reused, the partial shot is recaptured as a unit, and the final frame sequence has neither holes nor stale pixels.

#### Scenario: Corrupt a completed shot frame
- **WHEN** a PNG in a completed shot cache is missing, truncated or no longer matches its recorded hash or dimensions
- **THEN** that shot is not reused from the damaged generation, and the next successful output records the replacement generation rather than claiming a hit.

### Requirement: Incremental output is exact and honestly attributed

For the same validated inputs and environment, an incremental render SHALL produce the same PNG bytes at every output index as a fresh render; skipping cached shots MUST NOT change browser-state-dependent pixels in later captured shots. The output SHALL contain each global frame exactly once in order and preserve existing MP4, thumbnail and SRT semantics. The render manifest MUST report the current output hash and by-shot capture/reuse counts, key, frame range and originating generation/provenance; reused frames MUST NOT be attributed to the current capture run. A failed encode or interrupted publication MUST NOT leave a manifest that falsely identifies a partial output as complete.

#### Scenario: Cold comparison across a reuse gap
- **WHEN** a render reuses an earlier shot and then captures a later shot with model motion, source text, animated overlays and a transition
- **THEN** every incremental PNG hash equals the corresponding forced-cold PNG hash, including the first captured frame after the reuse gap, and the manifest distinguishes the earlier origin from the current capture.

#### Scenario: Encode fails before publication
- **WHEN** encoding or frame verification fails after some shots have been captured or reused
- **THEN** no new complete-output manifest claims the failed MP4, while verified shot generations remain eligible for a later render.
