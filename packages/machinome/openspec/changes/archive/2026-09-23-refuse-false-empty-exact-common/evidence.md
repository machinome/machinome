## Red-first and candidate verification

Before changing `machinome/exact.py`, `tests/test_exact_common_guard.py` failed at both direct `intersect_shapes` and `_exact_verdict`: a mocked false-empty native Boolean passed as clearance even for two overlapping, valid CadQuery boxes. With the guard, all seven focused tests pass, covering direct and managed refusal, disjoint and face/edge-only controls, unchanged native overlap and containment, section failure, and indeterminate classifier state. The new tests use actual CadQuery solids and OCCT section/classifiers; only the Boolean result or explicitly failing seam is patched.

The implementation source `machinome/exact.py` SHA-256 after the indeterminate-state correction is `9eb6be3b251ec92473a99096f884f323d6086e690e315a309bf38c7c4d8b2e93`. Official OCCT classifier semantics distinguish `Rejected()` (a successful quick OUT result) from `TopAbs_UNKNOWN` (indeterminate); the guard accepts the former as OUT and refuses the latter. A separate read-only review verified that translated-world boxes still produce a strict shared-interior witness and that a raised refusal is not memoized as a clearance verdict.

Using the originating project's unchanged `OperatingCurta` source shapes and this implementation, a +0.01 mm axial free control returns an empty common; the outer radial +0.2 and −0.2 mm axial cases each raise `ExactCommonInconsistency`; the side +0.2 mm control returns one native solid of volume 0.15265938435625293 mm³. This is direct framework-helper evidence, not a project-source mutation or an inferred volume for the inconsistent cases. The project's own production capture and interval gates are independently running; they are not claimed by this change's tests.

A small CPU-13 microcheck of 30 repeated face-tangent unit-box comparisons took 0.07676 s for `_boolean` alone and 0.88726 s through `intersect_shapes`, about 27 ms added per empty contact. This is an illustrative synthetic hot-pair cost, not a universal per-model overhead claim; correct broad-phase negatives do not reach the guard, and exact empty candidates can pay the extra section/classification work. The canonical whole-framework gate, `taskset -c 12 env OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q` with the workspace venv's absolute Python path, exited 0: **3595 passed, 4 skipped, 53 warnings, 2131 subtests passed in 429.54 s**. An initial `python -m unittest discover -s tests` invocation is not that gate: it imported nested fixtures as top-level modules and produced relative-import errors, so its 140 errors are not treated as a candidate regression.
