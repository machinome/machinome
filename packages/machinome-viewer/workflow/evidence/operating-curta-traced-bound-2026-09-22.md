The baseline finished that segment in **48.218 wall / 47.948 process-CPU seconds**, resolving **282,905,965** DAG nodes. The search-local candidate took **25.172 wall / 25.108 process-CPU seconds**, resolving **93,578,605** nodes: 47.8% less process CPU in this paired segment. Both exited 0 with 36° admitted, tick 96, generation 0, and the identical sorted 213-bank digest `3d63c86327099f82643b75ebe7f865d40177588ca1d77ee6b820c4a8b474696f`.

The trace and first-48 result establish exactness and a measured segment gain, but not a reliable active-tick speedup. Other host processes were active outside reserved CPU 15, so wall times are observations, not hardware-independent budgets. The full browser revolution is still unverified and remains much slower than its declared two-second drawing duration.
