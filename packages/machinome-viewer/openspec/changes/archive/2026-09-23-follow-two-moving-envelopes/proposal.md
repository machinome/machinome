## Why

The OperatingCurta's positioning ball is free between a bell surface that pushes it outward and a carriage collar that pushes it inward. Its current self-read switch law pulls the ball back when the bell retreats and stops a raised-carriage crank at zero; the existing Play edge has only one source and fixed clearance, so a viewer cannot faithfully consume the needed two independently moving envelopes. The producer is separately proving a narrow Follow edge for this actual project.

## What Changes

- Consume only the producer's accepted version-12 running Follow declaration: one retained bank follower between two authored moving boundary expressions. Project the follower through the complete source paths, retaining its position while neither surface pushes; preserve exact absolute landing, snapshot/restore and replay.
- Use the existing dynamic Bounds and complete prefix replay, augmented by certified Follow conflict candidates where uniform samples could miss a narrow incompatible interval, to stop a requested input at first contact. Reject malformed or uncertified boundary paths by name rather than approximate them with endpoints or silent sampling.
- Preserve every version-1–11 self-read law, Play edge, source timing and old snapshot interpretation. No old law is reinterpreted as Follow and no independent browser-side controller is invented.
- Keep this first Follow relation terminal: its retained target may be read by the required Bounds but shall not feed another compiled edge. Match the producer's first-operand tie behavior at signed zero rather than using JavaScript's native `Math.min`/`Math.max` ties.
- Advertise version 12 (and the corresponding viewer API capability) only after producer/consumer corpus parity, current Curta stop/relief/replay and a real browser control pass. This is a source capability change, not a package release.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `running-two-envelope-follow`: A retained coordinate follows two moving, certified boundaries without being pulled by retreat or crossing an incompatible interval.

### Modified Capabilities

- `viewer-package`: Accept and distinguish a well-formed version-12 Follow running document while retaining older document semantics.
- `viewer-distribution`: Report the new supported document/API capability only with a bundle that executes it.
