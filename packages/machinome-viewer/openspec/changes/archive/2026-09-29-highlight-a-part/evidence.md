## What changed

- `src/presentation.ts`: `MaterialPresentation`, the one writer of a
  material's `emissive`, `color`, `transparent`, `opacity` and `depthWrite`
  outside construction (design D3, ADR-076): base values recorded on first
  touch, presented values computed from the base and the standing overlays
  (hover, highlight), base written back and the record dropped when no
  overlay stands; `forget()`, `drop()`, `isGhosted()`. `highlightRequest`
  checks and copies a request (D6). `DEFAULT_HIGHLIGHT_COLOR`, `DIM_FACTOR`,
  `GHOST_OPACITY` and `HOVER_EMISSIVE` (moved from `viewer.ts` with its
  comment) are named constants.
- `src/viewer.ts`: `liftParts` is `presentation.setHover(...)` returning
  `() => presentation.setHover([])`, so the part surface's collaborator keeps
  its signature; `highlight(paths, options)` on the handle (D1, D4, D6),
  rendering after applying and after restoring; `forget()` before meshes or
  materials are replaced in `replaceTree`, `manifestChanged` and
  `artifactChanged`, and the standing highlight re-resolved from its paths
  afterwards, a vanished path dropped and an empty highlight lapsing (D7);
  `dispose()` drops the owner without writing; `partOnScreen`'s point search
  sees through ghosted meshes, `pickAt` stays unfiltered (D5).
- `docs/reference/viewer-handle.rst`: `highlight` after `setView`;
  `docs/compatibility.rst` names it under API 28; `tests/test_documentation.py`
  checks both.
- ADR-076 Accepted; ADR-053 records the amendment; the ADR index follows.

## Constants

Not retuned: **`#ffb000`, dim 0.35, ghost 0.15** are the values used. Every
pixel check passed with them on the first run:

- `#00ff00` on the hub (keep): the hub point's mean colour
  (157, 53, 54) -> (157, 255, 54); blade pixels identical; after restore the
  screenshot is byte-identical to the one before.
- Dim: blade b1 brightness (sum of channels) 140 -> 76, hub not darker.
- Ghost, blade b0 pointed at the camera over the middle of the hub
  (`setTime(0.75)`, camera `[2, -80, 2]`): the centre pixel reads blue before
  and (222, 161, 47) through the ghost; blade b1 brightness 235 -> 687 (toward
  the white page behind the transparent canvas); `partOnScreen(['Hub']).point`
  is that centre while ghosted and the grid point again after restore.

Frames inspected by eye (`_build/film-annotations/highlight-*.png` and
`highlight-sheet.png`, regenerated by this cycle's script, not committed):
keep, dim and ghost read clearly; the ghosted blade end shows the tinted hub
through it; a highlighted blue blade takes a pale amber glow beside dimmed
ones. The default amber, added as an emissive glow, largely evens out the
shading of the red hub; the reference says so rather than promising the part
keeps its shading.

## Deviations from the design

- `highlightRequest` also refuses a path that is not an array of names,
  naming it, before the tree is asked; D6 lists the other refusals only.
- The order of refusals is: disposed viewer, request (paths, colour,
  others), unknown or ambiguous path, then a standing highlight. All come
  before any change.

## Out of scope, found while applying

- `FullscreenE2ETest::test_exiting_restores_the_canvas_size_and_the_label`
  races: once `fullscreenElement` is null and the canvas has its old size it
  reads the button's `aria-label` at once, but the browser relabels the
  button only when it dispatches `fullscreenchange`, which can come later.
  Today it lost 4 of 4 runs against a bundle built from base `40031a5`, 2 of
  3 against the `track-a-part-on-screen` bundle and 3 of 3 then 2 of 3
  against this change's, so it is not this change's. Traced in a page: the
  label read "Exit full screen" at the moment of the exit and "Full screen"
  200 ms later, with the change event logged. The test now awaits the label
  (5 s) before asserting it; 4 of 4 passed after, and the whole suite with
  it. What the test proves is unchanged.
