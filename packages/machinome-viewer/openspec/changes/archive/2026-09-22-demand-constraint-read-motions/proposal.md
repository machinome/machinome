## Why

The committed OperatingCurta `7586002` export makes its crank high bound read a self-read, nonlinear anti-reversal pawl. The viewer currently demands traced motion from edge dependencies but not from bound reads; the pawl is omitted and the constraint search re-evaluates its subprogram for each sample. A declared two-second crank gesture remains far too slow to operate normally even after the prior expression improvements.

## What Changes

- Demand the motion of a coordinate read by a compiled constraint when the program can construct it, including a coordinate whose defining law also reads its own held value.
- Keep the existing law, sample sequence, bound arithmetic, stop result, bank, replay and refusal semantics. Prove any speed gain on the frozen export rather than assuming the framework result transfers to JavaScript.
- Add a viewer-owned red-first regression and exact actual-export evidence. If a new traced path changes any sample or error behavior, retain the present fallback and do not integrate the optimization.
