## Why

After `share-one-geometry-per-model-file`, one open copy of the Enigma I page (`enigma-i` at `081f4e6`) is 3,034 MiB in Chromium, and the 2,868 parts read from model files stand on 993 geometries of 536.7 MiB in the tab and the same again as WebGL buffers. Half of each is the `normal` attribute, which the viewer computes itself from the positions (`tree.ts` 51, `computeVertexNormals` on a geometry with no index: one normal a triangle, written on its three vertices), while a flexible part holds none and is drawn with three.js's `flatShading` (`flexible.ts` 141-147, 427-436). With the file parts drawn that way in an experiment on the page, one open copy was 2,489 MiB, the typed arrays and the WebGL buffers each 268.4 MiB less, and in nine states at most 508 of a picture's 605,864 pixels differed, each by one step of 255, but for one pixel by 11 in three of them (`workflow/evidence/enigma-rigid-part-normals-2026-11-22.md`; `workflow/warts.md`, "Every part has its own geometry and material, and an idle page draws all of it every frame", its last two entries). The first requirement of this change is that a part looks as it looks today; the second is the memory. Where the two cannot both hold exactly, the design says where and by how much.

## What Changes

- **No normals on a part read from a file.** The reading of a model file removes the `normal` attribute three.js's `STLLoader` makes, in the step where it now computes the normals, before any part has the geometry and before any copy is made for a second viewer. No geometry handed to a part has one, so none is uploaded. `computeVertexNormals` is no longer called on a file part's geometry.
- **Each facet lit by its own plane, as a flexible part's is.** The rigid kind of material (`RIGID`, coloured and colourless) is made with `flatShading: true`: three.js takes each fragment's normal from the screen derivatives of its view position, which for every facet the viewer draws is the normal of the facet's plane on the side facing the viewer, the same vector the stored normal gave. Hover, highlight, dim and ghost are clones of it and keep the flag. The two kinds `RIGID` and `FLEXIBLE` stay two, with the same count of materials, the same sharing and the same order of drawing.
- **The picture is equal within one step, with two named exceptions.** Measured for this proposal with the viewer's bundle built from the base and from a prototype of this design (reverted), in the suite's Chromium on hand-written model files and on the suite's marked fixture: 77 pictures (5.96 million pixels) of outward and inside-out cubes, spheres near and far, a 512-sided cylinder, four parts under every highlight mode, and the marked fixture, coloured and colourless, differ by at most one step of 255 in a channel (16,080 pixels by one step, none by more). Two cases differ by more, at a few pixels: a facet seen within about a pixel of edge-on (a row of 80 pixels by up to 14 coloured, 64 colourless) and triangles of nearly zero area (up to 40 coloured, 63 colourless). Flexible parts are drawn this way already. Both cases are the pilot's to accept (design, "For the pilot").
- **Markings unchanged.** A marking's decal is still welded, given averaged normals and lifted along them, from a copy of the file's geometry; its material keeps its stored normals, both sides and its polygon offset.
- **Nothing else moves.** Picking, part controls, bounds, the order of drawing, highlights, flexible parts, capture, the `on-demand` mode, the requests a page makes, ADR-082's sharing and its counts of holders, the mount handle, viewer API 30 and document versions 1 to 14.
- Tests red on the base: in the widget suite, the geometries, attributes and normal computations of a mounted tree's file parts and their materials' flag, after a mount, a reload, in-place updates and in a second viewer's copy, and in every look; in Chromium, the WebGL buffers and bytes a part costs, and a part's picture checked by what must hold within one run (each facet flat, lit on the side facing the viewer, never black, in every look). The comparison with the base's pictures is the reviewer's, made once and recorded as evidence.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `viewer-package`: a new requirement, "A part read from a model file is drawn from its positions alone", states that no normal is held for such a part, how its facets are lit, and where a pixel may be lit as from another direction. The requirement "The viewer draws the markings a part carries" is restated: its sentence that a node without markings is drawn "exactly as it is drawn by a viewer that has never read a marking" and its scenario "An unmarked document is untouched", which compares the picture with one "rendered before this viewer could draw a marking", compared with an earlier build of the viewer and are no longer exact against one; they now compare within one viewer. Design Decision 6 lists every sentence read and why the others hold.
