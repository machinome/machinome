## Why

When a clocked instruction or a drawn gesture is pressed, the viewer does inside the click's own task two tree updates and two renders, of which one update and one render produce a picture of the transition's end that is never shown; and the drawing then takes the click's own time from the motion, because its first frame advances by the animation loop's wall delta since the frame before the click. On the Enigma I page (`enigma-i` at `081f4e6`, measured script only on viewer `fd334597`), a wheel step's click takes 249 ms, 202 of it in those two updates, and the step, declared at 0.4 s, begins its first frame 0.25 s after the click at about 0.6 of its travel and lands on the second: the pilot sees it jump from start to end (`workflow/warts.md`, "A press draws the scene twice before its first frame, and its own time is taken from the motion"; `workflow/evidence/enigma-press-path-2026-11-20.md`).

## What Changes

- A request whose transition the viewer will draw (a pressed instruction of non-zero duration, and a gesture on a handle of the clocked panel) has its final pose **judged and not shown**: every transform the existing dependency rule selects is prepared and checked, and every flexible part it reaches has its parameters evaluated and, where they differ from the values its geometry was built from, is evaluated by molejo into buffers that are then discarded. Nothing is copied into the scene, no part's geometry or remembered values change, and nothing is rendered. A refusal by any of these refuses the request exactly as today, with the same message.
- Because the scene was never moved, the drawing starts where the scene stands: its first frame is measured against the bank the machine stood at before the request, moves nothing, and costs no tree update. The press poses nothing at the origin and renders nothing; the panel is rebuilt once, after the drawing's state is set, and reads the origin.
- The drawing's clock starts at its **first drawn frame**: the wall delta that frame carries (which contains the press) is not counted, and the declared duration is counted from there. Every later frame advances by its own wall delta as today.
- A frame of a drawing that moves no id poses nothing.
- The other doors are unchanged: the mount handle's `move`, `restore` and `reset`, the clock's transport and step, an instruction of zero duration, the panel's reset, landing a drawing, and every on-demand path still pose and render through the machine's hook before they return.
- A refusal of a drawn request's final pose by a flexible part's evaluator no longer leaves the rigid parts at the refused candidate, because nothing of the candidate is shown. The same refusal on a publishing door (the handle, the clock, a zero-duration instruction) still does; that pre-existing gap is neither widened nor addressed there.
- New red-first tests: in `clocked/drawing.test.ts` (the first frame is measured against the start, the drawing's clock), in `flexible.test.ts` and `color.test.ts` (judging a pose shows nothing and refuses as an update would), and a browser test on the committed calculator fixture (no draw call inside a drawn press, the first animation frame after a press reads the origin, also after a press that took 300 ms). One existing vitest expectation that pins the old first-frame diff is changed, as design Decision 9 states; one existing browser check (`GestureDrawnInABrowserTest` 10e) has a narrower margin, also stated there.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `viewer-package`: "The viewer executes a clocked machine's requests" states that a request the viewer will draw judges its final pose once without showing it; "The viewer plays a clocked instruction as one drawn transition" states that nothing shows the transition's end before the drawing's first frame, that a press renders nothing itself, that the declared duration is counted from the drawing's first frame, and that a drawn request refused by a flexible part's evaluator leaves every part where it stood. Every existing scenario of both requirements is restated unchanged.
