## Using the name and the marks

Anyone may, without asking:

- use the name Machinome and the unaltered marks to refer to Machinome: to
  say that a machine, a project, a course, a talk, a video, an article, a
  tool or a product is made with, built on, compatible with, or about
  Machinome;
- show the marks in presentations, articles, books, courses, videos,
  documentation and websites about Machinome, and among the logos of
  other tools in a list of what was used;
- credit Machinome in a film, a publication or a product's about page,
  with the wordmark or with the words "Made with Machinome";
- link to machinome.org.

On these conditions:

- the marks are used as the approved files give them: unaltered, in one of
