## Decisions

**D1. Wording follows the sibling's grant; history does not.** Every page a
reader lands on states the viewer's licence as it is now. `HISTORY.rst`'s
0.6 entry and the ADRs record what was decided when, and stay.

**D2. The test refuses the stale spelling rather than pinning the new one
everywhere.** A page that no longer mentions the viewer's licence is fine;
one that says `AGPL-3.0-only` is wrong, since nothing in this ecosystem the
manual describes is licensed that way.
